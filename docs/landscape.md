# Understand the career landscape

## What does “AI forward engineer” mean?

The employer title to investigate is **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)**. In the [Anthropic example](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5391016008-109), engineers work directly with customers on production AI applications. The posting includes travel and substantial prior experience; it is not an entry-level template.

“AI-forward” can describe an approach to work. It does not reliably identify one specific occupation. Search the full title and inspect the responsibilities.

## Three useful distinctions

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Building with models or building models? | Application engineering and model development need different portfolios. |
| Who is your user? | Customers, internal teams, and researchers have different delivery needs. |
| What outcome do you own? | A prototype, deployed service, evaluation suite, and roadmap are different jobs. |

## Emerging titles, established foundations

[Anthropic's careers board](https://www.anthropic.com/careers/jobs) includes applied engineering and architecture titles. [OpenAI's board](https://openai.com/careers/search/) includes specialized AI systems roles. This demonstrates varied naming, not total market growth.

Prompting, retrieval, tool integration, and evaluation can be skills within many jobs. Treat narrow titles such as “Prompt Engineer” as search terms to verify, rather than assuming a universal career ladder.

Compare five to ten postings in your target location. Record responsibilities, seniority, eligibility, travel, and required versus preferred skills. Use the [research worksheet](./job-research.md).
