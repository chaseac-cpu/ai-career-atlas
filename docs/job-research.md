## Compare a sample

### Worked application and agent comparison

These three existing examples were checked November 5, 2026. The skill paraphrases below follow the recorded required/preferred fields in the [directory data](https://github.com/chaseac-cpu/ai-career-atlas/blob/main/docs/data/jobs.json); inspect the linked employer description for exact wording and changes. The stage and experience differences matter: this is not one promotion ladder.

| Employer posting | Career stage / experience | Required or requested skills in the recorded sample | Preferred skills in the recorded sample | Editorial portfolio response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [NewsBreak: Applied AI Engineer, Advertising Agents (New Grad)](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/newsbreak/jobs/4700278006) | New graduate; explicitly no professional experience required; relevant degree/graduation gate | Software fundamentals; foundational LLM, retrieval and tool-use knowledge; structured outputs and APIs | Internships, research, advertising metrics, agent projects | Build a synthetic account-diagnosis agent with structured output, a dry-run mode, and a small evaluation set. |

| [Scale AI: Senior Frontier Agents Engineer (Applied AI)](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/scaleai/jobs/4720478005) | Senior; 5+ relevant engineering years; production LLM systems | Python, modern AI tooling, ML fundamentals, quantitative evaluation, customer communication | Production agents, retrieval/memory, cloud, workshops, model adaptation | Present an agent case study with ablations, failure analysis, deployment constraints, and a stakeholder walkthrough. |

Software foundations, integration, and measurement provide useful practice themes across these examples, but each employer asks for different depth. A preferred production-agent background is not interchangeable with a required years-of-experience gate. The portfolio column is our advice, not an employer requirement or proof of eligibility. These three purposively selected postings cannot measure market-wide skill frequency or demand. See the [selection limits](./sources.md#search-and-selection-log) and [career-stage rules](./opportunities/levels.md#our-classification-rules).

Gather five to ten relevant postings. Count repeated requirements and preserve title variants. Avoid inferring the whole labor market from a small sample.

Use the [real examples](./opportunities/index.md) as a model for recording details. Preserve both parts of an alternative such as “bachelor's plus experience OR master's.” Record total engineering years separately from years in AI. A visible application page alone does not establish that interviews are still being offered.

For compensation, preserve location, currency, level, base versus total compensation, and date. This guide does not publish salary rankings because its research does not support them.
