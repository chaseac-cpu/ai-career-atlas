## Interpret the guides

- **Observed title:** an employer uses the title or stated specialized variant.
- **Related observed title:** our heading groups related work; the employer uses the identified variant.
- **Editorial role family:** a way to organize career exploration, without claiming a posting uses that exact title.

Role summaries, comparisons, learning plans, and project ideas are editorial synthesis, not employer-approved descriptions. They do not guarantee employment.

## Limits

The original role guides are weighted toward major AI employers. The expanded job sample includes enterprise software, finance, identity, healthcare, consulting, industrial and aerospace organizations, and AI infrastructure. It remains a purposive sample, not a representative labor-market survey. It does not establish hiring volume, industry-wide growth, salary distributions, or entry-level availability. Job pages can change, close, or redirect. Original sources are linked; full descriptions are not reproduced.

## Job-example methodology

The [22-example collection](./opportunities/index.md) covers 18 employers and U.S. locations or explicitly U.S.-remote arrangements. Chrome was used for search and employer-page inspection, supplemented by direct primary-source page checks. Search snippets, AI-generated search overviews, and aggregators were discovery aids only; job cards rely on employer sites or their recruiting platforms.

Every card links its original description and records **November 5, 2026** as the check date. Each selected source displayed a description and an application link or form. We call that **application page visible**, not independently confirmed active hiring. We did not submit applications, verify recruiter capacity, or confirm future availability. A rolling deadline or an old page can coexist with a visible form.

The collection contains seven internships/co-ops, seven entry/early-career examples, four mid-level examples, and four senior/staff examples. Different roles at one company are distinct examples, not evidence of separate employers or hiring volume. Exact titles are preserved. Required, requested, preferred, and degree-alternative qualifications are kept distinct. The [classification rules](./opportunities/levels.md#our-classification-rules) explain editorial level assignments.

Compensation retains the displayed currency, unit, geography, and relevant caveats. We do not annualize hourly rates, convert an annualized internship salary into internship earnings, assume an unlabeled pay period, or rank ranges as equivalent offers. Benefits, equity, bonuses, and geographic bands differ. Unmentioned sponsorship or eligibility is unknown.

All job summaries are concise paraphrases. Portfolio ideas are our advice, not employer requirements. Data is maintained in `docs/data/jobs.json`; the individual cards and directory should be updated together when sources change.
