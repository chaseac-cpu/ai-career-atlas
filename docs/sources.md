## Interpret the guides

- **Observed title:** an employer uses the title or stated specialized variant.
- **Related observed title:** our heading groups related work; the employer uses the identified variant.
- **Editorial role family:** a way to organize career exploration, without claiming a posting uses that exact title.

Role summaries, comparisons, learning plans, and project ideas are editorial synthesis, not employer-approved descriptions. They do not guarantee employment.

## Limits

This small source set is weighted toward major AI employers. It does not establish hiring volume, industry-wide growth, salary distributions, or entry-level availability. Job pages can change, close, or redirect. Original sources are linked; full descriptions are not reproduced.

## Contribute

Open a pull request with an employer URL, exact title, date checked, and explanation of what changed. Separate source observations from advice. Do not include recruiter messages or personal applicant information.
