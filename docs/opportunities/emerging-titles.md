# Emerging titles and changing AI work

These are observed names and responsibilities in the November 5, 2026 sample. We have not established when each title originated or whether its hiring volume is growing. “Emerging” describes the work this guide investigates, not proof that a job was just invented.

| Observed wording | What it adds to the career map | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Native Engineer | Application engineering using both AI capabilities and AI-assisted development | [Valtech](./entry-level.md#valtech-junior) |

| AI Application Engineer focused on inference | Serving and hardware/storage measurement rather than only application features | [Samsung](./internships.md#samsung-inference-intern) |

## Some specialties are responsibilities, not separate titles

Context engineering, evaluation, observability, guardrails, retrieval, and AI enablement appear inside broader job descriptions. Searching only for a narrow title can miss relevant work. For example, the [Impiricus AI Engineer](./mid-level.md#impiricus-ai) description includes harnesses and evaluations without making those the job title.

Likewise, “AI Engineer” may mean internal operational tooling, a customer feature, enterprise knowledge retrieval, or model infrastructure. Compare [DV Trading](./internships.md#dv-devops-intern), [NewsBreak](./entry-level.md#newsbreak-applied-newgrad), [Implicit](./mid-level.md#implicit-applied), and [Air](./senior.md#air-senior-ml).

## Broaden your searches

Try role words such as **applied AI, forward deployed, AI enablement, agent systems, evaluation, inference, AI platform, LLMOps, AI-native**, and **internal tools**. Pair them with **intern, co-op, university, new graduate, junior**, or a target location.

These are suggested search terms, not claims that each is a standardized occupation. This first collection is strongest in engineering. Governance, research science, design, and nontechnical AI operations need a separate sample before making comparisons about their entry routes.
