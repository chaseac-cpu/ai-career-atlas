# How responsibilities change with level

Titles and years of experience are imperfect signals. Compare the employer's stated level with the scope of work and the qualifications. The following is an editorial comparison grounded in the linked examples, not a universal promotion ladder.

| Stage | Scope illustrated by this sample | Real example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internship / co-op | A bounded project, mentoring, and a fixed academic term; sometimes substantial technical prerequisites | [DV Trading](./internships.md#dv-devops-intern) |
| Entry / early career | Contributing to systems with support; degree windows or prior internships may apply | [NewsBreak Applied AI](./entry-level.md#newsbreak-applied-newgrad) |
| Mid level | Owning a component or use case through deployment and operation | [BPD](./mid-level.md#bpd-mid-ai) |
| Senior / staff | Architectural choices, reusable platforms, evaluation strategy, and broader technical direction | [Scale General Agents](./senior.md#scale-general-agents) |

## Compare similar work, not just similar words

In the agent/application family, an [intern at Schonfeld](./internships.md#schonfeld-enterprise-intern) can deliver a scoped use case, a [new graduate at NewsBreak](./entry-level.md#newsbreak-applied-newgrad) can contribute components, and a [senior engineer at Scale](./senior.md#scale-frontier-senior) can own agent architecture and experiments across customer deployments. These are different companies and contexts, not successive jobs on one guaranteed ladder.

Infrastructure has its own path: the [Samsung internship](./internships.md#samsung-inference-intern) focuses on serving benchmarks; [NewsBreak's ML infrastructure role](./entry-level.md#newsbreak-ml-infra) covers production pipelines; [Air's senior role](./senior.md#air-senior-ml) owns broader model-lifecycle infrastructure.

## Our classification rules

1. Preserve the exact advertised title, including Intern, New Grad, Jr., Senior, or Staff.
2. Record the experience wording and any degree alternative separately.
3. Use an explicit employer level when one is given. BPD states “mid-level.”
4. If the title is unlevelled, label our grouping as editorial and explain its basis. A 3+ requirement alone does not prove a standardized level.
5. Keep experience-gated junior examples in early career with warnings, rather than presenting them as first jobs.
6. Treat a Senior/Staff posting as a multi-level opening, not a single seniority point.

For example, [LTS](./senior.md#lts-harness-senior) requests a longer engineering background plus a shorter AI specialization. Do not read only the AI-specific number. The research does not infer seniority from salary.
