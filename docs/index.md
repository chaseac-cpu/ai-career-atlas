## A job title is only the starting point

An AI Engineer may build a product feature. A Forward Deployed Engineer may work inside a customer's business. An Evaluation Engineer may measure whether either system works. Start with the work you want to own, then investigate the employer's version of the role.

New to the field? Read the [career landscape](./landscape.md), [compare roles](./roles/index.md), then choose a [portfolio experiment](./portfolio.md).

An independent research snapshot and learning resource, not a live jobs feed. Role summaries and learning plans are editorial synthesis. Verify current requirements on the employer's website.
